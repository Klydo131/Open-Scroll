# Code of Conduct

Open Scroll is built for churches of many traditions, by people who may worship very differently from one another. We ask everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space) to treat one another the way they would wish to be treated.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to technology.
- Respect other traditions. Disagree about code and design, not about anyone's faith.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work in front of us.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Mocking or belittling anyone's beliefs, background, identity, age or ability.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including details of a church or its members.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Repeatedly derailing discussion after being asked to stop.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people from the project when this code is broken. Report a concern privately through the repository's **Security** tab, choosing **Report a vulnerability** and noting in the title that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
